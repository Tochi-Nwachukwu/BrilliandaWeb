The core

Results and records,covered completely.

This is what Brillanda is for, and what every other part of the product serves. From the day a student is admitted to the day their final report card is downloaded.

Student records

Admission numbers, guardians, photos and status, with class placement tracked per session so every past year stays intact.

Spreadsheet import

Upload what you already keep. Map your column names to ours, see exactly which rows failed and why, and import the clean ones anyway.

Sessions & terms

Two semesters or three terms, your call. Roll classes and subjects into a new session in one action instead of rebuilding each August.

Classes, arms & subjects

JSS 1 with its arms, and the subjects that apply at each level, so Further Maths never appears on a JSS 1 report card.

Grading & weighting

Your CA-to-exam split and grade bands, different by class level if you need. Gaps and bad weights are caught at setup, not at publish.

Score entry

Keyboard-first, autosaving, and strict about what it accepts. A score above the maximum is refused at the cell, not found at report time.

Results & positions

Weighted totals, grades and rankings recomputed across the whole class every time a score changes, with ties handled one consistent way.

Report cards

A whole class in one action, on your letterhead, with your grading key and the class teacher's words. Downloadable again next year.

Review & publishing

Entry, locked, reviewed, published. An explicit gate between a teacher finishing and a parent seeing, reversible if you spot a mistake.

Audit trail

Every score change, override and publish recorded with who, when and what it was before. Append-only — nobody can edit the record.

Parent & student portals

Read-only, published terms only, and never a word about any other student. Designed at phone width first.

Insights & export

Grade distribution, subject comparison and term-over-term trends, plus a full data export you can take whenever you like.

Beyond the core

Four more, whenyou're ready for them.

These sit alongside results and records rather than competing for attention with them. Switch on the ones your school actually needs and ignore the rest — nothing here changes how score entry works.

Attendance Module

Mark a whole class present in one tap, flag latecomers, and let the day's totals build themselves. Termly attendance can print on the report card alongside results, or stay separate — your call.

Timetables Module

Build a clash-free weekly grid per class that respects which teacher is where, then adjust by hand where reality disagrees. Teachers see only their own periods.

AI assistant Module

Ask in plain English and get an answer from your own data — who hasn't entered scores, which class average moved, who's below the pass mark in Basic Science. It reads and drafts; it never changes a score on its own.

CBT exams Module

Objective tests on screen with a timer and a question navigator, marked the moment they're submitted. Runs over the hall's own Wi-Fi when the internet is down, and the scores post straight into the grid as an assessment component.

Still not in Brillanda, on purpose: school fees and accounting, payroll and HR, and a public website builder. Those are real jobs that deserve real software, and bolting on a weak version of each is how school systems end up with thirty modules and four good ones.

Questions

Answered honestly.

Including the ones where the answer isn't what you were hoping for.

Book a walkthroughBook a walkthrough
What's the difference between the core and the extra modules?
The core — records, score entry, results, report cards and publishing — is what every school gets and what we hold to the highest standard. Attendance, timetables, the AI assistant and CBT sit alongside it and are switched off until you want them. Nothing in the extras changes how score entry or computation behaves, which is deliberate: the part your term depends on shouldn't shift because you enabled a timetable.
Does it handle school fees?
No. Fees, payroll and accounting are real jobs that deserve real software, and a weak version bolted on is how school systems end up with thirty modules and four good ones. If fee collection is your main problem today, we're honestly not your system.
Our grading scale isn't the standard one. Can we change it?
Yes. Set your own grade bands, remarks and CA-to-exam split, with different rules for junior and senior classes if you run them differently. We pre-fill the common West African scale so you can start today, and we catch gaps at setup — a score between 39 and 40 that maps to nothing gets flagged before it can break a report card.
We have years of records in Excel. Do we retype everything?
No. Upload the spreadsheet and match your column names to ours in a dropdown — if you called it "Surname" and we call it "Last name", you fix that in the interface rather than re-editing the file. Rows with problems are listed with the exact reason, the clean ones still import, and the failures download as a corrected template. On paid plans we do the first migration with you.
Does the AI assistant change our data?
It reads and it drafts, but it doesn't change a score on its own. Ask who hasn't entered yet, which class average moved, or who's below the pass mark, and you get an answer from your own data. Anything that would alter a record still goes through the same review and audit trail as a human doing it by hand — a machine shouldn't be able to quietly move a child's grade.
Our internet is unreliable. Will teachers lose their work?
Each score saves on its own as it's entered rather than waiting for a submit button, and a failed save is queued and retried instead of dropped. CBT exams run over the exam hall's own Wi-Fi when the internet is down, and sync when it returns.
What if a wrong score gets published?
Unpublish, correct, publish again. Every change is recorded with who made it, when, and what it was before. We'll also say plainly that parents who already opened the results may have seen them — pretending otherwise would cost you more than the mistake.
What happens to our data if we leave?
You export everything — students, staff and every term's results — as spreadsheets, at any time, without asking us or giving notice. It's your school's record. Holding it hostage would be a worse business than earning the renewal.
