Results & records

We do one thing completely, rather than ten things partly.

The end of term is the busiest and most error-prone week in a school's year. Three staff with calculators, checking each other, losing days to arithmetic that a machine should have done.

Brillanda takes the scores your teachers already record and carries them all the way to a parent's phone — computed, checked, approved by you, and kept for as long as the student is yours.

Built for the school laptop you already have.

Scores save as they're typed, so nothing is lost when the connection drops halfway through third period.
