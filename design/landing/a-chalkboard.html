A term in four moves

Set it up once in August. The other three happen every term, and only the second one takes real time.

1

Set up

Enter your classes, subjects and grading scale. We pre-fill the standard scale so most schools just check it and move on.

2

Enter

Teachers fill in a grid that behaves like the spreadsheet they already use — but won't let them save a score of 150 out of 100.

3

Review

Lock the term, check the computed results, and see flags on anything that looks wrong before a parent ever sees it.

4

Publish

Approve once. Parents get results and downloadable report cards the same minute.

Everything results-related, nothing else

Brillanda does one job completely instead of ten jobs partly.

A grid teachers recognise

Students down the left, assessments across the top, keyboard to move between cells. The same shape as the mark sheet on their desk.

Positions without the argument

Totals, averages and class positions computed the same way every term, with ties handled consistently. No one recalculates by hand.

Report cards in one action

Generate a whole class at once, with your logo and your grading key. Parents download the same PDF you approved.

Nothing goes out unapproved

Teachers enter. You review and lock. Only when you publish does anything reach a parent — and every change is on the record.

Parents check on their phone

No paper slips, no queue at the office, no "my child lost it". A parent signs in, sees the term, downloads the report card. That's the whole experience.

What Brillanda handles

The whole path from a teacher's score to a parent's phone.

  • Student and staff records, imported from your spreadsheets
  • Your grading scale and CA-to-exam split, however you run it
  • Score entry, computation, positions and report cards
  • Review, approval and publishing to parents
  • Every past term, kept for as long as the student is yours

What it doesn't — on purpose

We'd rather do results properly than everything poorly.

  • School fees and accounting
  • Timetabling and scheduling
  • Attendance registers
  • Online lessons and assignments
  • Chat between staff and parents

The document parents actually keep

Your logo, your grading key, your class teacher's words. Generated for a whole class in one action, and downloadable again next year when someone misplaces theirs.
