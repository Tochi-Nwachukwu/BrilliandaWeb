A term in four moves

Set it up once in August. The other three happen every term — and only the second one takes real time.

1

Set up

Enter your classes, subjects and grading scale. We pre-fill the standard scale, so most schools just check it and move on.

2

Enter

3

Review

Lock the term and check the computed results, with flags on anything that looks wrong — before a parent ever sees it.

4

Publish

Approve once. Parents get results and downloadable report cards the same minute.

Everything you need to know

Including the things Brillanda deliberately doesn't do. We'd rather do results properly than everything poorly.

Book a walkthrough
Does it handle school fees, attendance or timetables?
No — and that's deliberate. Brillanda does results and student records completely rather than doing ten modules partly. Fees, attendance and timetabling are the things schools most often tell us are half-built in the systems they're leaving. If you need an all-in-one, we're honestly not the right fit yet.
Our grading scale isn't the standard one. Can we change it?
Yes. You define your own grade bands, remarks, and the split between continuous assessment and exam — down to different rules for junior and senior classes. We ship the common West African scale pre-filled so you can start immediately, but every part of it is yours to edit.
We have years of records in Excel. Do we retype everything?
No. Upload your existing spreadsheet and match your columns to ours — if you called it "Surname" and we call it "Last name", you fix that in a dropdown rather than re-editing the file. Rows with problems are listed with the exact reason, and the clean rows still import.
What if a teacher enters a wrong score after we've published?
You can unpublish, correct and publish again. Every change is recorded with who made it, when, the old value and the new one. We'll also tell you plainly that parents who already opened the results may have seen them — we don't pretend an unpublish is invisible.
Our internet is unreliable. Will teachers lose their work?
Each score saves as it's entered rather than waiting for a submit button at the end, and a failed save is queued and retried rather than dropped. The grid is deliberately light so it works on a modest connection and an older school laptop.
If we stop using Brillanda, what happens to our data?
You export everything — students, staff and every term's results — as spreadsheets, at any time, without asking us. It's your school's record, not ours. We'd rather earn the renewal than hold your data hostage.

Try it on one class this term

Set up a single class, let one teacher enter their scores, and see the report cards come out the other end. If it doesn't save you the week, don't keep it.
