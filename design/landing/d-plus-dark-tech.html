Questions, answered honestly

Including the ones where the answer isn't what you were hoping for.

Does it handle fees, attendance or timetables?
No, and that's a deliberate choice rather than a gap we're rushing to fill. Results and records are the part schools most often tell us is half-built elsewhere, so we do that completely. If an all-in-one system is what you need today, we're honestly not it.
Our grading scale isn't the standard one. Can we change it?
Yes. Set your own grade bands, remarks and CA-to-exam split, with different rules for junior and senior classes if you run them differently. We pre-fill the common West African scale so you can start today, and we catch gaps at setup — a score between 39 and 40 that maps to nothing gets flagged before it can break a report card.
We have years of records in Excel. Do we retype everything?
No. Upload the spreadsheet and match your column names to ours in a dropdown — if you called it "Surname" and we call it "Last name", you fix that in the interface rather than re-editing the file. Rows with problems are listed with the exact reason, the clean ones still import, and the failed ones download as a corrected template to fix and re-upload. On paid plans we do the first migration with you.
Our internet is unreliable. Will teachers lose their work?
Each score saves on its own as it's entered rather than waiting for a submit button at the end, and a save that fails is queued and retried instead of dropped. The grid is deliberately light so it works on a modest connection and an older staff room laptop.
What if a wrong score gets published?
Unpublish, correct, publish again. Every change is recorded with who made it, when, and what it was before. We'll also say plainly that parents who already opened the results may have seen them — we don't pretend an unpublish is invisible, because pretending would cost you more than the mistake.
Can teachers see other teachers' classes?
No. A teacher sees only the class and subject pairs they're assigned to, enforced on the server rather than hidden in the interface. Admins see everything in their own school and nothing in anyone else's.
Do students see their class position?
Only if you switch it on. Student access is off by default, and position display is a separate toggle — some schools want it on the report card but not in the portal. Neither parents nor students ever see anything about another student.
What happens to our data if we leave?
You export everything — students, staff and every term's results — as spreadsheets, at any time, without asking us or giving notice. It's your school's record. Holding it hostage would be a worse business than earning the renewal.
How long does setup actually take?
One class, about ten minutes. A whole school with several hundred students and a spreadsheet to import is usually an afternoon. We'd rather you set up a single class first and test it with one teacher before committing the whole school.

Something we haven't covered? Email us — a person answers.

Try it on one class this term.

Set up a single class, let one teacher enter their scores, and see the report cards come out the other end. If it doesn't save you the week, don't keep it.
