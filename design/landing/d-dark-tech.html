What Brillanda does

Three jobs, done completely: the scores go in, the results come out, and the parents get them. Nothing else in the way.

Score entry

Students down the left, assessments across the top — the same shape as the mark sheet already on their desk.

A grid that behaves like a spreadsheet
Tab moves across, Enter moves down the column — the way teachers actually enter a set of scores. Sticky student names and a live total per row, so nobody loses their place at row 34.
Refuses what a spreadsheet would accept
A score above the component maximum is rejected at the cell, not discovered at report time. The maximum sits in the column header so it never has to be remembered.
Saves as they type
Every score saves on its own rather than waiting for a submit button at the end. A failed save is queued and retried, so a dropped connection halfway through third period costs nothing.
Absences and notes
Mark a student absent for one component and it's excluded from the weighting properly. Add a note to any cell — "resat 14 Nov" — so the reason stays with the score instead of in someone's memory.

Results & positions

Every total, grade and position computed the same way, every term — with the workings on the record.

Weighted totals, your split
Define your CA-to-exam split once — 40/60, 30/70, or per class level — and every subject total follows it. Weights that don't sum to 100 are blocked before they can produce a wrong result.
Your grading scale, not ours
Set your own grade bands and remarks. We pre-fill the common West African scale so you can start today, and flag any gap — a score between 39 and 40 that maps to nothing is caught at setup, not at publish.
Positions, with ties handled
Subject and overall positions recalculate across the whole class as scores change, using one consistent ranking rule. Nobody recalculates by hand, and nobody disagrees about whose child came third.
Flags on anything odd
A total of zero, a missing score, a class average that moved 25 points since last term — surfaced for you to check before publishing. Advisory only; they never block you.

Report cards & publishing

Nothing reaches a parent until you've reviewed it — and when it does, it's one action for the whole class.

A whole class in one action
Generate every report card for a class at once, with your logo, your grading key and your class teacher's remarks. Under two minutes for a class of thirty-four, and downloadable again next year.
Review, then lock
Once a teacher marks a subject complete, lock it so it can't drift while you're checking. Every change to a score is recorded with who made it, when, and what it was before.
Publish when you're ready
One action makes results visible to parents and enables their downloads. Publish per class rather than all-at-once, and unpublish if you spot a mistake — we'll tell you plainly that some parents may already have seen it.
Parents check on their phone
No paper slips, no queue at the office, no "my child lost it". A parent signs in, sees the term, downloads the card. Parents with more than one child switch between them from one account.

A term in four moves

Set it up once in August. The other three happen every term — and only the second one takes real time.

1

Set up

Enter your classes, subjects and grading scale. We pre-fill the standard scale, so most schools check it and move on.

2

Enter

Teachers fill in the grid. It behaves like the spreadsheet they already use, but won't accept 150 out of 100.

3

Review

Lock the term and check the computed results, with flags on anything odd — before a parent ever sees it.

4

Publish

Approve once. Parents get results and downloadable report cards the same minute.
