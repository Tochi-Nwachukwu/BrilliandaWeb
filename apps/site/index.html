What is in it today

Six things, done properly.

This is the whole of version one. We would rather these six work every day than have twenty that half work.

Score entry

A sheet per subject and arm. Type a score, see the total and grade immediately. Entries save on their own, and keep trying if the network drops.

Grades and positions

Your own grading scale, your own CA/exam split. Positions are worked out within the arm, and a tie gives both students the same position.

Report cards

The school’s own header, the term’s scores, remarks and the attendance you record. Printed in a batch or downloaded one at a time.

Parent portal

Parents sign in and read the result for each of their children. Invited by email, or given a printed access code if they have no email.

Publishing control

Nothing reaches a parent until the school says so. Publish a whole class or a single student, and unpublish if something needs fixing.

A record of changes

Every score change is kept with who made it and when. If a mark is queried next term, the history answers it.

How a term runs

From an empty session to a published result.

Set the session up once. Every term after that is the last four steps.

01

We set your school up

Send us your classes, arms, subjects and staff list. We create the school and the first admin account, then hand it over.

02

Your admin adds the students

Students, their arms and their parents. Parents are invited by email, or given a printed access code if they have none.

03

Teachers enter their scores

Each teacher sees only the subjects and arms assigned to them. They mark a sheet complete when it is done.

04

The school reviews and publishes

The proprietor sees which sheets are still outstanding, then publishes a class or a single student. Parents get the result the moment you do.

Questions

Before you ask.

Can we keep our own grading scale?
Yes. You set the grade letters and the mark each one starts at, and the CA/exam split. It applies to the whole session, so a term already published is not disturbed.
What happens when a teacher has no data?
Scores save on their own as they are typed. If the connection drops mid-sheet, the entry is kept and sent again when the line comes back, and the teacher can press save at any time.
Can a parent see another family’s child?
No. A parent account is linked to specific students, and the system will not return a record for any other child, in your school or in anybody else’s.
What if a parent has no email address?
The school prints an access code for that parent. They use it once to set up their sign-in, and after that the code is spent.
A score was published with a mistake. Now what?
The teacher asks for the sheet to be unlocked, the school approves it, the correction is made and the result republished. The change, and who made it, is kept on the record.
Do we need fast internet?
No. The screens are deliberately light and each score is sent on its own, so a slow or unsteady connection slows you down rather than stopping you.
